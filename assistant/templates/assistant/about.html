About SmartHealth Assistant

An intelligent, web-based health information platform engineered to deliver preliminary symptom analysis, health condition insights, and actionable precautions using transparent rule-based heuristics.

Project Objective

SmartHealth Assistant is an MCA (Master of Computer Applications) academic project designed to demonstrate how a modern web-based health information system can process user-provided symptoms and provide preliminary informational guidance.

The platform bridges the gap between raw medical symptom lists and structured health education by computing normalized Symptom Match Scores, filtering emergency medical conditions, and advising timely consultation with qualified doctors.

Clinical Safety Protocol

A core design principle of SmartHealth Assistant is adherence to strict healthcare safety boundaries:

  • Zero False Diagnosis: Scores represent symptom overlap matching, never a statistical disease likelihood or formal diagnosis.
  • Dedicated Emergency Triage: Symptoms such as severe chest pain, loss of consciousness, or breathing distress immediately bypass standard condition matching to trigger urgent care alerts.
  • Transparent Rule-Based Inference: Deterministic logic ensures predictable, explainable matching behavior suitable for academic examination.

How the Symptom Matching Algorithm Works

The inference engine executes deterministic set evaluation across the symptoms database:

Symptom Match Score Formula:
Match Score (%) = (Number of Matched Condition Symptoms / Total Symptoms in Condition Profile) × 100

Example: If a user selects 4 symptoms, and 3 match the 5 defining symptoms of "Flu-like Illness", the Match Score is (3 / 5) × 100 = 60.0%.

Conditions are ranked in descending order of their Match Scores. Only conditions with at least one matching symptom are displayed to the user.
